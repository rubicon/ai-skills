# Media Briefs

A brief, not a decoration. If a visual does not carry information the text cannot, it does not earn
the space.

## Carousel / document post

6-10 slides. One idea per slide.

```
SLIDE 1   The hook. Must work as a thumbnail at phone size.
SLIDE 2   The problem or the stakes.
SLIDE 3-8 One point each. Roughly 15 words maximum per slide.
SLIDE 9   The synthesis.
SLIDE 10  One next step, or the source.
```

- **Readable at phone size.** This is the constraint that kills most designs. Large type, high
  contrast, minimal decoration
- **One idea per slide.** A slide with three ideas is three slides
- **Consistent structure** across slides so the reader learns the pattern
- **No logo on every slide.** Once, at the end

Best for: frameworks, step sequences, before/after comparisons, checklists. Poor for: narrative
argument, which reads better as prose.

## Data visuals

- **Real numbers from the ledger only.** Never an illustrative chart with invented values, even
  labeled as illustrative — screenshots outlive their captions
- **Source on the visual itself**, not only in the caption
- **One comparison per chart.** A chart making three points makes none
- **Axes labeled and starting at zero** unless there is a stated reason otherwise
- **Anonymize client data** unless publication is explicitly authorized

## Single images

Specify what it must show and why it earns the space. Screenshots of real artifacts — a dashboard,
a whiteboard, a document — outperform stock imagery by a wide margin because they are evidence.

Never: stock photography of people in meetings · handshakes · abstract technology imagery ·
AI-generated images of people, which are increasingly recognizable and read as low-effort to this
audience.

## Video

- Under 90 seconds for feed video
- Captions always — most viewing is muted
- The first three seconds decide whether it plays
- A talking-head clip from a real talk beats a produced piece for credibility

## Brief format

```
Visual: carousel, 8 slides
Purpose: make the MarTech consolidation argument scannable
Constraint: must read at phone width

Slide 1  "14 tools. 6 questions we still couldn't answer."
Slide 2  What the stack cost: $1.4M, 3-week reporting lag
Slide 3  The audit: what each tool was actually used for
...
Slide 8  "Six tools. Two days. Same questions, now answered."

Data: from imports/martech-consolidation-readout.pdf. Tool names anonymized -
client hasn't published the vendor list.
```

Name the source and the anonymization decision in the brief. That decision is easy to lose between
brief and production, and it is the one that matters.
